Community Guidelines

TalentGoldPlus is built to create opportunities, encourage respect and protect the wellbeing of our community.

1. Respect Others

Treat all users with respect. Harassment, bullying, intimidation, threats, hate speech or discrimination will not be tolerated.

2. Be Honest

Users must provide accurate information about their identity, role, qualifications, experience, sporting background and services.

3. Protect Young People

Extra care must be taken when interacting with young athletes. Adults must behave professionally and appropriately at all times.

4. No Scams or Misleading Offers

Do not post fake opportunities, false trials, misleading investment offers, fraudulent services or unrealistic promises.

5. Appropriate Content

Do not upload or share content that is abusive, explicit, violent, exploitative, illegal or harmful.

6. Marketplace Behaviour

Marketplace listings must be genuine, clear and honest. Users are responsible for carrying out proper checks before entering into agreements.

7. Reporting Concerns

If you see behaviour that breaches these guidelines, report it to TalentGoldPlus using the available reporting tools or by contacting us.

8. Moderation

TalentGoldPlus may remove content, restrict access or suspend accounts where users breach these guidelines or put others at risk.
