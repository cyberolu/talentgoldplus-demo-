Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how TalentGoldPlus uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit and improve your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • To keep you securely signed in.
  • To remember your preferences and settings.
  • To improve website performance and reliability.
  • To understand how users interact with the platform.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Required for core platform functionality such as login, authentication and security.

Performance Cookies

Help us understand how visitors use TalentGoldPlus so we can improve the platform.

Preference Cookies

Remember your choices such as language or interface preferences where applicable.

4. Third-Party Services

TalentGoldPlus uses trusted third-party services including Firebase for authentication, database storage and hosting. These services may use cookies or similar technologies as part of their operation.

5. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of TalentGoldPlus.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
