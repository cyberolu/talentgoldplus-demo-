Safeguarding & Child Protection

TalentGoldPlus is committed to providing a safe, respectful and inclusive environment for every member of our community, particularly children and young people.

1. Our Commitment

We believe every athlete has the right to participate in sport in a safe environment free from abuse, bullying, harassment, exploitation and discrimination.

2. Young Athletes

TalentGoldPlus welcomes young athletes using the platform with the support of parents, guardians, schools, clubs and responsible adults where appropriate.

3. Expected Behaviour

  • Treat every young person with dignity and respect.
  • Communicate professionally at all times.
  • Never request inappropriate images or personal information.
  • Never exploit your position of trust.
  • Report safeguarding concerns immediately.

4. Reporting Concerns

Any safeguarding concern should be reported immediately to TalentGoldPlus. Where appropriate, concerns may also be referred to relevant safeguarding authorities or law enforcement agencies.

5. Platform Action

TalentGoldPlus reserves the right to suspend or permanently remove any account that presents a safeguarding risk or breaches these standards.

6. Working Together

Safeguarding is everyone's responsibility. Parents, coaches, clubs, professionals and athletes all play an important role in creating a safe sporting environment.
