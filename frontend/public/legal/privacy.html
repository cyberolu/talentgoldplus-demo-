Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TalentGoldPlus collects, uses and protects personal information.

1. Who We Are

TalentGoldPlus is a sports platform that connects athletes, coaches, professionals, scouts, investors and partners.

2. Information We Collect

  • Name, email address and account details.
  • Profile information such as role, sport, location, biography and achievements.
  • Uploaded media including profile images, photos, certificates and video links.
  • Messages, notifications, marketplace listings and community activity.
  • Technical information such as device, browser and usage data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To display your profile and connect you with other users.
  • To operate messaging, marketplace, media and community features.
  • To improve safety, security and platform performance.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide support.

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. Some profile information may be visible to other registered users as part of the platform.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

6. Children and Young People

TalentGoldPlus may be used by young athletes. Parents, guardians and responsible adults should support younger users when creating profiles or sharing information online.

7. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal data.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: talentgoldplus@gmail.com
